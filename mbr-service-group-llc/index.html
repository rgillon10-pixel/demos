What we clean

Every exterior surface, done the right way for that surface.

Vinyl, stucco, brick, cedar, pavers, concrete, composite and asphalt shingles all need different pressure and different detergents. We match the method to the material so nothing gets damaged.

House washing

Soft wash for siding, trim and gutters. Removes green algae, black streaks, spider webs and pollen without blasting the paint.

Roof soft washing

Those black streaks are algae eating your shingles. Low-pressure treatment that kills it at the root and keeps your warranty intact.

Driveways, walkways, pavers

Questions

The things people ask before they book.

How much does house washing cost?

Most single-family homes in Commack fall in a predictable range and we quote the exact number from a quick look at the house, usually the same day you call. The price is written and it does not change.

Will pressure washing damage my siding or roof?

High pressure would, which is why we do not use it there. Siding and roofs get a low-pressure soft wash with detergent that does the work. Driveways and concrete get the pressure they can take.

Do I need to be home?

No. We need access to an outdoor spigot and we ask that windows are closed and cars are moved off the driveway. You get before and after photos by text.

How long until it gets dirty again?

A house wash typically holds 18 to 24 months on Long Island. Roof treatments last several years. Sealing pavers roughly doubles the time between cleanings.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully. Ask and we will send the certificate before the job.
